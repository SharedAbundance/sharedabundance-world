Thank you for joining. One more step: invite two people.

Your membership becomes active once two people you invite have joined. This is how Shared Abundance grows: person to person, through people who know each other.

How Help us grow works

  1. You tell us who to invite. Add the name, email and mobile number of two people below.
  2. We send each of them one invitation. One WhatsApp message and one email, with your name, so they know it comes from you.
  3. We tell you when each one joins. When both have joined, your membership becomes active.
  4. If someone hasn't joined after a week, we let you know so you can follow up with them personally.
  5. If they still haven't joined after a month, we remind you once more. After that, we leave it.
We only contact each person you invite once. They can simply ignore the message, and we will not chase them.
