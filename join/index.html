Membership

Join Shared Abundance

Members are the people the shared surplus belongs to. One person, one equal share.

Joining is free. Fill in the form and we will send you an email to activate your account.

Your membership becomes active once two people you invite also join. We call this step Help us grow.

Sign up

It takes about a minute. We only use your details to run your membership and keep you updated.

Invited by a member. Their invite code is filled in for you.

What happens next

  1. Check your email. Click the link we send to activate your account.
  2. Invite two people. Tell us who you would like to invite. We send each of them one invitation.
  3. Become active. Your membership becomes active once both of them have joined.

Questions? Email info@sharedabundance.world.
